Orbiq – Privacy Policy

App: Orbiq – Number Merge Puzzle (package com.orbiq.game) · Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Last updated: 7 October 2026

Summary

Orbiq is a puzzle game. It shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and does not create user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We do not sell any data.

1. Data stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores on your device: your boards, scores, best score, highest orb, level stars, Stardust balance, power-ups, daily reward and streak status, missions, player level, unlocked themes and trails, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders). This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google provides leaderboards, achievements and a cloud save. Your Play Games profile name may appear on leaderboards. The cloud save contains only your game progress (as listed in section 1) and is stored in your Google account by Google. This processing is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can stop using Play Games at any time in the Google Play Games app.

3. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.

ServiceWhat is collectedWhy
Firebase AnalyticsGame events with numbers only (for example: game started, game over with score and highest orb, level up, power-up used, mission completed), an app instance ID, and basic device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country derived from the network).Improve game balance and features.
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and an installation ID.Find and fix crashes.

We have turned off collection of the advertising ID and ad personalization signals. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, not shared with advertisers, and not used for advertising. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Firebase keeps analytics data for a limited time (by default up to 14 months) and Crashlytics data for 90 days. Learn more in Firebase privacy.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules at most one local reminder per day about the game (daily challenge, missions or streak). Reminders are created on your device, do not contain advertising, and can be turned off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: Play Games, cloud save and analytics. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Notifications and run at startup: show your daily reminder and keep it after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

6. Children

Orbiq is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and deleting data

Because the game has no accounts, there is no account to delete. You can delete all on-device data by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. You can delete your Play Games data from the Google Play Games app. To ask about analytics data, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes

If we change this policy (for example, if a future version adds ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
