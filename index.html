Orbiq – Privacy Policy

App: Orbiq – Number Merge Puzzle (package com.orbiq.game) · Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy is written in English. The English version is the official one; the app shows it with a translated note in Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Korean and Turkish.

Summary

Orbiq is a puzzle game. It shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and has no sign-up. We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We do not sell any data. The game plays fully offline; only the optional online features below send data.

1. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores on your device: your boards, scores, best score, highest orb, level stars, Stardust balance, power-ups, daily reward and streak status, missions, player level, unlocked themes and trails, your profile (the nickname you choose, your avatar and frame), your own records (My Records), and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders, language and time zone). Your language and time zone choice stay on the phone and are never sent. Except for the Star League data in section 2, this data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Star League (daily leaderboard)

When you finish a Classic game, or leave one that is still in progress (pause, go Home or switch apps), Orbiq sends your best score of the day to the Star League, which runs on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in the United States, region us-central1). The following is sent and stored:

When you are offline, your scores wait on your device and are sent automatically when you are back online. Star League data is kept until you delete it (section 8). Firebase processes this data for us as a service provider under the Firebase terms.

2b. Invite friends

Every player gets a personal invite link (https://orbiq-aeb4c.web.app/i/CODE). To give the right rewards, Orbiq stores in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States):

  • your invite code with your anonymous player ID and nickname;
  • when someone installs or opens Orbiq from your link: their nickname, avatar ID, whether they were a new player, the time, and a one-way hash of their phone's Android ID. Only the owner of the link can see this list (nickname, avatar, status, time).
  • a one-way hash of each phone's Android ID that has been seen, so that reinstalling Orbiq can never count as a new player (fraud prevention). The original ID is never sent and cannot be recovered from the hash.

Which link installed the app is read from Google Play (Install Referrer). Rewards are Stardust inside the game only. Delete this data with Settings → Delete my leaderboard data (your code, your friends list and your rows on other players' lists) or by email (section 8).

3. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google provides leaderboards, achievements and a cloud save. Your Play Games profile name may appear on leaderboards. The cloud save contains only your game progress (as listed in section 1) and is stored in your Google account by Google. This processing is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can stop using Play Games at any time in the Google Play Games app.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes.

We have turned off collection of the advertising ID and ad personalization signals. Analytics data is not linked to your identity, not shared with advertisers, and not used for advertising. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Firebase keeps analytics data for a limited time (by default up to 14 months) and Crashlytics data for 90 days. Learn more in Firebase privacy.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules at most one local reminder per day about the game (daily challenge, missions or streak). Reminders are created on your device, do not contain advertising, and can be turned off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: the Star League (sending scores when you are online), Play Games, cloud save and analytics. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Notifications and run at startup: show your daily reminder and keep it after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

7. Children

Orbiq is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and deleting data

  • Star League, in the app: open Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. This immediately deletes your scores from every daily board, releases your reserved nickname and deletes your anonymous player ID (needs an internet connection). Your progress and records on the phone stay. If you play Classic again later, you join with a new anonymous ID.
  • Star League, by email: you can also request deletion by writing to moonbytelabs.support@gmail.com with the subject "Orbiq Star League data deletion", your in-game nickname and the days you played. We delete the matching Star League data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Data on your device: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Play Games: delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.
  • Analytics: analytics data is not linked to your identity; for questions, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy (for example, if a future version adds ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
