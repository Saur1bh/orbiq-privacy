Orbiq – Privacy Policy

Orbiq – Number Merge Puzzle (Android, package com.orbiq.game) by Moonbyte Labs · Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy is written in English, which is the official version. The app also shows a short translated note in Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Korean and Turkish.

Orbiq is a relaxing number-merge puzzle game. We built it to be fun without collecting more than it needs. This policy explains, in plain words, what data the game uses, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and how you can delete it.

At a glance

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account or sign-up.
  • Never collected: your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • Never sold, rented or shared with advertisers. The advertising ID is turned off.
  • The game plays fully offline. Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • Online features (Star League, invite friends, sharing, Google Play Games) use only a nickname you choose and a random anonymous ID.
  • You can delete your online data at any time from the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data.

1. Who we are

Orbiq is made by Moonbyte Labs (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the data described here. You can reach us at moonbytelabs.support@gmail.com.

2. Data saved on your phone

So you can continue where you left off, the game saves on your phone: your boards and scores, best score and highest orb, level stars, Stardust and power-ups, daily reward and streaks, missions, player level and XP, unlocked themes and trails, your profile (the nickname you choose, your avatar and frame), your own records (My Records) and your settings (sound, voice, music, vibration, reminders, language and time zone).

This data stays on your phone. Your language and time zone choices are never sent anywhere. Everything is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Only the data described in sections 3 to 7 ever leaves your phone.

3. Star League and nicknames

The Star League is a daily worldwide leaderboard. When you finish a Classic game, or leave one that is still running (pause, go Home or switch apps), the game sends your best score of the day. It runs on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, servers in the United States).

No email, real name, contacts or location are sent. If you are offline, scores wait on your phone and are sent automatically when you are back online. Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity is used to block fake requests and cheating; it checks that requests come from the real Orbiq app.

4. Invite friends

Every player gets a personal invite link (https://orbiq-aeb4c.web.app/i/CODE). When a friend installs Orbiq from your link, you both earn Stardust inside the game. To give the right rewards, the game stores in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States):

  • your invite code, with your anonymous player ID and nickname;
  • when someone installs or opens Orbiq from your link: their nickname, avatar ID, whether they were a new player, the time, and a one-way hash of their phone's Android ID. Only you, the owner of the link, can see this list (nickname, avatar, status and time);
  • a one-way hash of each phone's Android ID that has been seen, so that reinstalling Orbiq can never count as a new player (fraud prevention). The hash is made on the phone; the original ID is never sent and cannot be recovered from the hash.

Which link installed the game is read from Google Play (Install Referrer). Rewards are Stardust inside the game only; there is no money or real-world prize.

5. Sharing your results

You can share a score, a completed level, a Daily Challenge win, your Star League rank or your best. The share image is created on your phone. You choose the app to send it with (Android share menu), or copy the text yourself. Nothing is sent to us when you share.

The image shows your score, highest orb and the result you are sharing. It shows your nickname and avatar only while “Show my name” is on (you can turn it off before sharing). The text includes your invite link, so friends who install from it count as your invites (section 4). What happens to a shared image afterwards is decided by the app you send it with and the people you send it to.

6. Google Play features

Google Play Games (optional). If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google provides leaderboards, achievements and a cloud save of your game progress (section 2) in your Google account. Your Play Games profile name may appear on its leaderboards. You can stop using it at any time in the Google Play Games app.

Ratings and updates. The game may ask Google Play to show its rating card, and may show a Google Play update notice when a new version is available. Both screens are shown by Google Play; we never see your rating text or Google account.

These features are provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game shows at most one reminder a day about the game (Daily Challenge, missions or your streak). Reminders are created on your phone, are not sent from a server, never contain ads, and can be turned off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

9. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: the Star League, invites, Google Play Games, analytics and crash reports. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Notifications, run at startup and wake lock: show your daily reminder at the right time, also after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

10. Service providers

We use only Google services, which process data for us under their terms: Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication, App Check, Analytics, Crashlytics, Hosting for invite links) and Google Play (Play Games, Install Referrer, ratings and updates). Firebase data is stored in the United States. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase. We do not sell or rent data to anyone.

12. Security

All online data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Strict server rules allow each player to change or delete only their own data, accept only valid scores and keep everything else closed. Firebase App Check helps make sure requests come from the real app. No method of storage or transfer is perfectly secure, but we use these protections to keep your data safe.

13. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your online data in the game: Settings → Delete my leaderboard data. This immediately deletes your scores from every daily board, releases your reserved nickname, deletes your invite code and friends list (and your rows on other players' lists) and deletes your anonymous player ID. It needs an internet connection. Your progress on the phone stays. If you play Classic again later, you join with a new anonymous ID.
  • Delete by email: write to moonbytelabs.support@gmail.com with the subject “Orbiq data deletion”, your nickname and the days you played. We delete the matching data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Stay offline: the game works fully without the internet; nothing is sent while you are offline.
  • Hide your name when sharing: turn off “Show my name” on the share screen.
  • Turn off reminders: in Settings, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • Google Play Games: manage or delete it in the Google Play Games app.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. To use any of these rights, email us; we answer within 30 days.

14. Children

Orbiq is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the online features, email us and we will delete that data.

15. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses data (for example, if it ever adds ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
